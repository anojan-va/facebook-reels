You check their profile. You don't even want to. Your thumb just does it. You read old messages you already know by heart. And part of you feels a little bit crazy. Because it's been weeks. Maybe months. Why is this one person still living in your head?

Here's what I want you to know before we go any further. You're not crazy. You're not weak. Your brain is doing exactly what it was built to do. And once you understand why, you can finally start to turn the volume down. I'm going to show you how I did it, including the one mistake that kept me stuck for almost a year. Stay with me, because the last part is the one that actually set me free.

A few years ago, there was someone I'll call Lena. We were never even officially together. That's the embarrassing part. Three months of late-night talks, inside jokes, almost-dates. And then one day she just... stopped. No fight. No explanation. She started seeing someone else, and that was it.

And I couldn't let it go. I'd be at work, in the middle of a meeting, and suddenly I was thinking about something she said in August. I'd hear a song in a supermarket and my whole day was gone. My friends were patient for about two weeks. Then they started giving me that look. You know the one. "Are we really talking about her again?"

What made it worse was that I didn't understand it. If we'd been together for five years, sure, that would make sense. But three months? I felt pathetic. And that shame made me think about her even more.

It took me a long time to understand what was actually going on. And the first piece of the puzzle was something I learned almost by accident.
