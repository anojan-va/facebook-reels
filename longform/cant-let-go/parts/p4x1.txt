But understanding it is only half of it. You also need to know what keeps it going. Because there are a few things almost all of us do that feed the obsession without realising it.

The first one is checking. Their profile, their stories, their last seen, the old photos. Every time you look, you give your brain a tiny hit of the very thing it's craving. It's like trying to quit sugar while keeping a jar of cookies on your desk and opening it "just to look".

I know people say "just block them", and I used to think that was childish. It's not. Blocking or muting isn't about them. It's about protecting your own brain while it heals. You can always unblock later, when it doesn't hurt anymore. And funny enough, by then, you usually won't even want to. And while you're at it, archive the chat. Move the photos into a folder you don't open. Not because the memories don't matter, but because you can't heal a wound you keep pressing on.
