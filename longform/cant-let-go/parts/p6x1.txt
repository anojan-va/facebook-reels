So how do you actually let go?

Here's what I learned, and this is the part I wish someone had told me at the start.

You have to close the loop yourself. You will probably never get the explanation you want. They might not even know why they left. So stop waiting for them to give you an ending, and write it yourself.

I did this literally. I wrote Lena a letter. Three pages. Everything I never said. What I felt, what hurt, what I was grateful for, and that I was done waiting. And then I didn't send it. I read it out loud once, and I deleted it. It sounds dramatic, but something in my chest unclenched that night. For the first time, the story had a last page.

The second thing is to give the craving somewhere else to go. When the urge to check hits, have a plan ready. Go for a walk. Call a friend. Do ten push-ups. Anything that moves your body and gets you out of that loop. Your brain needs new rewards, not just the absence of the old one.
