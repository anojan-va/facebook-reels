And the last thing, the one that actually set me free. Fill the space they left with your own life. Not with a new person straight away. With yourself. Your friends, your goals, the hobbies you dropped when you were busy waiting for a reply. Because the real reason someone takes over your mind is that there's too much empty room in it.

For me it was running. I started because I couldn't sleep, and I needed to be tired. Three months later, I signed up for my first half marathon. Somewhere around mile nine, I realised I hadn't thought about Lena all day. Not once. I wasn't trying not to. There just wasn't room anymore.

And here's the thing nobody tells you about getting over someone. You don't wake up one day and decide it's over. You just notice, one random Tuesday, that they weren't the first thing you thought about this morning. And then it's a week. And then you hear that song in the supermarket, and you smile, and you keep walking. That smile is how you know. Not that you forgot them. That they finally became a memory instead of a wound.
