The third is to stop fighting the thoughts. This sounds backwards, but trying not to think about someone is like trying not to think about a pink elephant. The harder you push, the louder it gets. So when they pop into your head, just notice it. "There's that thought again." Let it pass like a car going by. Don't chase it, and don't slam the door on it either. Some days it'll pass a hundred times. That's fine. Every time you let it go by without following it, the road gets a little quieter.
