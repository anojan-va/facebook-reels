At the airport, the planes can still fly. But booking, check in, and flight planning all run on networks. In July twenty twenty four, one bad software update grounded thousands of flights around the world. And the internet was still on that day.
Stock markets shut down. In twenty fifteen, a computer glitch froze the New York Stock Exchange for almost four hours. Now imagine a week.
But here's the good news. Your lights are still on.
Power grids run on their own control systems, separate from the public internet. So your fridge keeps humming, your taps still run, and you can still make toast.
