So by day two, the shelves start looking like the end of a party. Bread, gone. Water, gone. Toilet paper? You already know.
Delivery drivers sit in their vans with no addresses. Somewhere, your parcel is just driving around in circles, like a lost puppy.
At the petrol station, the card readers are down too. The queue for cash only fuel goes around the block.
The banks are open, but they can't do much. The cash machines outside are offline. And in some countries, like Sweden, cash is so rare that lots of shops don't even take it anymore.
