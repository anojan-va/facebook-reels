## Description
Your alarm goes off. Good news: the alarm still works. Bad news: the entire internet, everywhere on Earth, just went dark, and it's staying dark for one whole week.

In this video, you live through it day by day: card machines that say DECLINED, empty supermarket shelves, a blue dot on a blank map, neighbours who suddenly have names, the 1969 message that crashed after two letters, and the moment on day seven when you realise the hardest part isn't losing the internet. It's getting it back.

Real events mentioned: the July 2024 software update that grounded thousands of flights, the 2015 New York Stock Exchange freeze, Egypt's 2011 internet shutdown, the October 2021 Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp outage, undersea cable faults (around 150–200 a year), and phantom vibration syndrome (Drouin et al., 2012).
