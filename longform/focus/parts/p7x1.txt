And the last one is my favourite. When the urge to check your phone hits, and it will, don't fight it, and don't follow it. Just notice it.

Say to yourself, "There's the urge." Then count slowly to ten.

Most of the time, by the time you get to ten, it's gone. An urge is like a wave. It rises, it peaks, and it falls on its own, if you don't feed it.

Remember what I said at the beginning? Most people try to force themselves to focus harder. That's why it doesn't work. You can't beat a habit with willpower alone. You beat it by changing what's around you, and practising the opposite.

When I started doing this, the first week was honestly uncomfortable. I felt restless. I kept reaching for a phone that wasn't there. But around the second week, something changed. I read forty pages in one sitting for the first time in years.
