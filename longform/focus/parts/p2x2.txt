Forty-seven seconds. That's how long the average person stays on one thing before jumping.

And here's what got me. A lot of those switches weren't caused by a notification. Nobody interrupted them. They interrupted themselves.

Think about that for a second. You open your email, nothing new. You check the news, nothing new. You open the same app you closed thirty seconds ago. Nothing new. And you do it again anyway.

That's the first thing you need to understand. Your attention isn't broken. It's trained. Every time you switch, your brain gets a tiny reward. Something new. Something different. A little hit of "what's this?" And anything you repeat thousands of times a day becomes a habit.

You've been practising switching for years. You're really, really good at it. You just never practised staying.
