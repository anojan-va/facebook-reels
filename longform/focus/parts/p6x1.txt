So what do you actually do?

The first thing is the easiest, and it works better than any app. Put your phone in another room. Not face down. Not in a drawer next to you. Another room. Remember the study. Distance is the whole point. If you're waiting for something urgent, turn the ringer on and you'll hear it.

The second thing is to train boredom on purpose. Start small. Ten minutes a day where you do nothing. A walk without headphones. Waiting in line without your phone. Sitting with your coffee and just drinking it.

It will feel strange. You'll feel the itch. That itch is the whole point. Every time you sit through it, you're teaching your brain that a slow moment isn't an emergency.
