The third reason is the one nobody wants to hear. You've forgotten how to be bored.

Think about the last time you just waited. In a line, at a red light, in the two minutes before a meeting starts. No phone. No podcast. Just you and your thoughts.

For most people, it's been years.

Every empty second now gets filled. Waiting for coffee, scroll. Ad comes on, scroll. Brushing your teeth, podcast. Even in the bathroom, we bring a screen.

I used to think that was efficient. Why waste two minutes? But those two minutes were never wasted. That's where your mind used to wander, plan, daydream, and come up with ideas. And we've handed every single one of them to an app.
