The fourth thing is how we rest. Or rather, how we think we rest.

You work for twenty minutes, you feel tired, so you take a break. And what does the break look like? Your phone. Videos, news, messages.

But that's not a break for your brain. That's more input. Faster, louder, more colourful than the work you just left. So when you go back, the work feels even slower and even more boring than before. You've just made the contrast worse.

It's like taking a break from running by sprinting.
