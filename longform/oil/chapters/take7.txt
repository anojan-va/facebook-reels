Now the farms are in trouble.
Modern farming runs on diesel. Tractors plough. Harvesters harvest. Trucks carry the grain away. Water pumps keep crops alive.
Fertilizer mostly comes from natural gas, so some of it can still be made. But getting it to the fields? That needs diesel too.
Some farmers bring back horses. Others try to convert tractors to run on electricity or on fuel made from plants. But you can't rebuild a whole farm system in a month.
Food gets more expensive every single day. Meat becomes rare, because feeding animals takes huge amounts of grain, and moving that grain takes fuel.
People start planting vegetables on balconies, in parks, and in their front gardens. Just like during the world wars.
Meanwhile, the stuff we already have is slowly wearing out.
