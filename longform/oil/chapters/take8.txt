Every engine, every machine, every wind turbine needs lubricant to keep moving. Most of it comes from oil.
Car tyres are made with synthetic rubber, mostly from oil. Roads are paved with asphalt, which is made from oil too. Potholes start appearing, and nobody can fill them.
Even your wardrobe changes. More than half of the fibres in our clothes are polyester, which is basically plastic. Cotton and wool suddenly become very fashionable.
And think about your phone. Its case, the coating on its wires, even parts of its screen use chemicals made from oil. Making new electronics suddenly gets much harder.
But humans are good at one thing in a crisis. Getting creative.
Factories that made petrol cars start retooling for electric ones. Engineers dust off old ideas, some of them more than a hundred years old.
And that's where things start to turn around.
Because one thing still works almost everywhere. The power grid. And anything that can plug into it suddenly becomes priceless.
