Plastic is made almost entirely from oil and gas. Packaging. Bottles. Phone cases. Car parts. And no new oil means no new plastic.
Hospitals feel it first. Syringes, gloves, IV bags, tubes. Most are plastic, and they're used once and thrown away.
Many medicines are made with chemicals that come from oil too. Even simple painkillers.
So hospitals start doing something they haven't done in decades. Washing and sterilising equipment, and using it again.
Around the world, roughly eighty percent of trade moves by sea. Now the ships just sit there. Phones, medicines, spare parts, all stuck in ports.
