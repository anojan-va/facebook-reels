Horses on the streets next to electric buses. Sailing ships in the harbour next to giant batteries.
It turns out we've lived without oil before. The first commercial oil well was drilled in Pennsylvania in eighteen fifty nine. Before that, people lit their homes with whale oil and candles.
Now, every country races to replace oil with something else.
Countries like Brazil already run millions of cars on ethanol, made from sugarcane. Fuel made from plants becomes the new gold.
