Ambulances stop in the middle of the road. Fire engines can't leave the station. Police cars sit where they were parked.
Millions of people walk home in the dark, leaving their cars behind. Some of them are hundreds of kilometres from home.
At sea, around fifty thousand big cargo ships suddenly lose power. They drift in the dark, carrying the world's food, fuel and phones.
Many freight trains stop too. In a lot of the world, the big locomotives that pull them run on diesel.
