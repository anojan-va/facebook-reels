Without trucks, fresh food rots in fields and warehouses, while people in cities go hungry. Governments start rationing, and the army uses its last vehicles that still move.
Rubbish starts piling up on the streets, because garbage trucks run on diesel too. After a few days, cities start to smell.
Fire brigades and ambulances share the few electric vehicles that exist. Doctors and nurses cycle to work.
Then we discover how much of our world is made of oil.
