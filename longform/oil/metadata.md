## Description
At midnight, every drop of oil on Earth vanishes. The crude underground, the tanks at every port, the fuel in every truck, ship and plane, and the petrol in your car.

In this video we fast forward through time: one hour, one day, one week, one month, one year and ten years. Planes glide (like the real 1983 "Gimli Glider"), highways freeze, ships drift, supermarket shelves empty, and we discover how much of our world is secretly made of oil: plastic, medicine, tyres, roads and even our clothes. And by the end, you'll see why the thing that saves us might already be plugged into your wall.

Facts used: ~100 million barrels of oil used per day, ~1.5 billion vehicles, oil makes only ~3% of world electricity, more than 9 in 10 units of transport energy come from oil, ~80% of world trade moves by sea, more than half of clothing fibres are polyester, the first commercial oil well (Pennsylvania, 1859), Brazil's sugarcane ethanol, coal-to-liquid fuel (Germany in WWII, South Africa today).
