Here's a strange truth. The more you try to make someone miss you, the less they actually do.

You've been there. You're the one who texts first. Every time. You're the one who plans, who checks in, who remembers the little things. And if you stopped, just for a week, you're scared of what you'd find out. That they wouldn't even notice.

So you look it up. How to make someone miss you. And everything you find is a game. Wait three hours before you reply. Post a picture where you look happy without them. Go cold. Make them jealous. Disappear and see if they chase you.

I tried most of those. And I'm going to save you the trouble: they work for about two days. Then you're back where you started, except now you're also exhausted from pretending.
