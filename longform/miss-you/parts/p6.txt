Now let's talk about the hardest part. What do you do when they pull away?

This is where most of us lose it. They take longer to reply, and the panic kicks in. You double text. Then triple text. You ask, "Is everything okay?" three different ways. You start analysing every emoji like it's a secret code.

I know that feeling in my chest. But chasing someone who's pulling away is like running after a bus that's already left. You look desperate, you get out of breath, and the bus doesn't stop anyway. And pulling away isn't always about you. Sometimes people are stressed, busy, or going through something they haven't told anyone. Your panic turns their quiet week into a problem they now have to manage.

What works is the opposite. Stay calm. Stay kind. Say something simple and honest, once. Something like, "Hey, I've noticed we've been a bit distant. No pressure, I'm here if you want to talk." And then go live your life. Really live it. Go to the gym, call your friends, finish that project.

The first time I actually did this, I expected it to backfire. Instead, she called me two days later and said, "You're different lately. In a good way." I hadn't changed anything about how I felt about her. I'd just stopped begging the relationship to prove itself every hour.
