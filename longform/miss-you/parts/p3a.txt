And that brings me to the second thing, the one that actually made the difference. People don't miss you because you're gone. They miss you because of what's missing when you're gone.

Think about that for a second. If someone spends a weekend without you, what exactly are they losing? If the honest answer is "someone who agrees with everything and is always free," there's nothing to miss. But if you're the person who makes them laugh until it hurts, who notices when they're quiet, who drags them into some ridiculous adventure, that's a real hole when you're not there.

So the question isn't "How do I make them miss me?" The question is "What am I bringing that's worth missing?"
