Most of our cities are now forests and swamps. If you walked through New York, you would see hills covered in trees, and every so often, a strange stone shape poking through the leaves.
That's because stone lasts far longer than steel or wood. Granite, marble, and the hardest concrete hold out the longest.
Our roads are gone, buried under soil and roots. Our dams have cracked and given way, and rivers have found their old paths again.
Somewhere, a forgotten cellar still holds glass bottles, perfectly fine. Glass can last for an incredibly long time.
Your phone? It's long gone. Its battery leaked, its circuits corroded, and its screen cracked under the soil. But its gold and copper are still down there, waiting.
