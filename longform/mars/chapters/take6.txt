And then there's something you can't see at all. Radiation.
Mars has no global magnetic field and a very thin atmosphere, so far more radiation reaches the ground than on Earth.
NASA's Curiosity rover measured it. Every day on the surface, you get roughly as much radiation as dozens of chest X rays.
That's why the smart move is to live underground, or to cover your habitat with a thick blanket of Martian soil.
