Next problem. Food.
You'll be growing some of it yourself, in a greenhouse with special lights. Lettuce, radishes, and yes, potatoes.
But you can't just plant them in Martian dirt. The soil contains chemicals called perchlorates, which are toxic to humans. You'd have to wash the soil first, or grow everything in water.
Farming on Mars is less "peaceful countryside" and more "chemistry exam you can eat."
