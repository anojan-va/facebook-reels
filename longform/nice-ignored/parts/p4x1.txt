The second thing I learned is about value. When everything is a yes, your yes stops meaning anything.

Think about it. If someone always agrees with you, their agreement doesn't feel special. If someone is always available, their time doesn't feel valuable. If someone praises everything you do, their praise becomes background noise.

I used to compliment everyone, all the time. I thought it made people feel good. But a friend once told me, "When you say something's great, I never know if you mean it." That stung. Because she was right. I'd given away so many compliments that they'd lost their value.

The people who get attention are often the people whose approval feels earned. Not because they're cold, but because their yes is real. When they say "That's brilliant," you know they mean it. So start being honest with your praise. Save it for the things you really mean. You'll be amazed how much more weight your words carry when they're not handed out like free samples.
