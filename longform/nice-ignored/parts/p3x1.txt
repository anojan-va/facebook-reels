And here's the part that really surprised me. Being too nice can actually make people trust you less.

In 2010, two researchers, Craig Parks and Asako Stone, ran an experiment where people played a group game sharing resources. Some players were secretly programmed to be extremely generous, giving a lot and taking almost nothing. You'd think everyone would love them. Instead, most people wanted them kicked out of the group.

When the researchers asked why, people said things like, "They make the rest of us look bad," or, "What are they really after?" Extreme generosity didn't feel safe. It felt suspicious.

When I read that, I thought about every time I'd done too much for someone who barely knew me. I thought I was being lovely. They were probably wondering what I wanted.
