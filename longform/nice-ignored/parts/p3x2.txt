And honestly, sometimes I did want something. I wanted them to like me. I wanted them to notice me. I was giving with an invisible bill attached, and getting quietly angry when nobody paid it.

A lot of nice people do this without realising. I'll be nice to you, and in return you'll like me, appreciate me, choose me. But nobody signed that contract. So when it isn't honoured, you feel used, and they have no idea why you're upset. I spent years feeling like the world owed me for all the favours I'd done. The truth is, nobody asked me to do most of them.
