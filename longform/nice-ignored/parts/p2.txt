It took me years to understand the answer. And the first thing I had to learn was that there's a difference between being nice and being kind.

Nice is about being liked. Kind is about doing good. They look the same from the outside, but they come from completely different places.

When I was being nice, I wasn't really thinking about the other person. I was thinking about how they saw me. I said yes because I was scared of what would happen if I said no. I agreed with everyone because disagreeing felt dangerous. My niceness wasn't a gift. It was a strategy to be accepted.

And people can feel that. They might not be able to put it into words, but they sense it. Something feels a little off. A little too eager. A little empty. Like talking to someone who's always waiting for your reaction before they decide how to feel.

Kindness is different. A kind person helps you because they want to, and says no when they need to. A kind person tells you the truth even when it's uncomfortable. Kindness has a spine. Niceness usually doesn't.
