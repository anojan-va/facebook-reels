Here's what happened when I started living like this. I lost a few people. The ones who only liked me because I was useful drifted away. And honestly, that hurt. But the people who stayed started treating me differently. They asked my opinion. They invited me first. They respected my time, because I finally respected it.

And years later, I ran into Chloe at a friend's wedding. We talked for a while, and at one point she laughed and said, "You're so different now. You actually tell me when I'm wrong." She meant it as a compliment. And for the first time, I realised I didn't need it to be.

So if you're a nice person who keeps getting ignored, I don't want you to stop being good. The world needs good people. I want you to stop being nice and start being kind. Have opinions. Say no. Stop apologising. Let people be disappointed. And give because you want to, not because you need something back.
