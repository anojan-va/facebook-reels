## Description
You remember everyone's birthday. You stay late to help. You never cause drama. And somehow you're the one who gets ignored.

It's not because you're too good. It's because niceness and kindness come from completely different places, and people can feel the difference. In this video I share what I learned the hard way: why "nice" quietly reads as less trustworthy, why handing out yeses and compliments makes them worthless, why having no edges makes you invisible, and the four things that changed how people treat me.

Research mentioned:
• Parks & Stone (2010), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology: unusually generous group members were the ones people wanted to kick out.
• Susan Fiske (Princeton): we judge people on warmth and competence. Nice people score high on warmth but low on strength.
