The fourth reason is one I see everywhere, especially in dating. Being too available.

I'm not talking about playing games. I hate games. But there's a difference between being kind and being instantly, completely, always available.

In 2011, researchers Erin Whitchurch, Timothy Wilson and Daniel Gilbert showed women the profiles of men who had supposedly looked at their profile too. Some were told the men liked them a lot. Some were told the men liked them an average amount. And some were told nobody knew how much the men liked them. Guess which men they thought about most, and found most attractive? The uncertain ones.

A little uncertainty keeps the mind busy. When everything is guaranteed, the brain stops paying attention.

I learned this the embarrassing way. I used to reply within seconds, cancel my own plans the moment someone texted, and agree with everything. I thought I was being a good friend. In reality, I had no life of my own to bring into the friendship. And people could feel it.

So have a life that doesn't revolve around anyone. Your own hobbies, your own goals, your own friends. Not to look busy. To actually be interesting. When you have somewhere to be, people want to know where you're going.
