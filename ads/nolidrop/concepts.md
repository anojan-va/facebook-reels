# NoliDrop: 3 ad concepts (for approval before any voice/image spend)

Shared across all 3 ads
- Hero: **Noli**, the glossy 3D blue water drop. Generated with Klein from the description until the mascot image
  arrives, then every Noli shot uses that image as its reference.
- **Signature moment, "the Drop":** Noli falls into frame and lands with a splash (SFX "bloop" plus a ripple ring).
  The ripple turns green water blue. It appears in every ad.
- **Line people repeat:** *"Don't guess. Noli knows."*
- End card in every ad (about 2.5 s): Noli waves, the **NoliDrop** wordmark, a "Get it on Google Play" badge
  (official badge style, drawn in code) and the VO line "NoliDrop. Get it on Google Play."
- App screens are rebuilt as clean vector UI in code (Home, Analysis results, PRIORITY #1, range bar 6.2–8.4 with
  7.2–7.8 in green, "Fix my pH with Noli"). Only the real features: water analysis, fix-order priority,
  "I have a problem" (green or cloudy water), start-up and winterizing guides, avoiding unnecessary chemicals.
  No stats, savings figures or reviews.
- Captions sit in the safe zone, there's a cut or visual change every 1–2 s, an SFX on every transition, ducked
  music, and the mix is at -14 LUFS.

## Ad 1: "The Receipt" (problem → solution), about 26 s
**Look:** the brand's sunny Mediterranean pool palette: bright cyan, white and sunlight. Bold rounded sans in white
with one cyan keyword and a brush underline, as in the Instagram posts. Transitions are splash wipes and receipt
paper tears. Energetic, polished female voice. The music is a bright upbeat pop/house bed.

| t | Visual | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | HOOK: a receipt prints fast into the camera: SHOCK $24.90, ALGAECIDE $22.90 … then a red stamp **TOTAL $124.80** (the receipt from the brand's own post) | "A hundred and twenty-four dollars…" |
| 1.5–3 | Smash cut: a top-down pool turns murky green with particles | "…and your pool is *still* green?" |
| 3–6 | Chemical tubs pile up and wobble, with "?" bubbles: pH? chlorine? shock? | "Most pool owners guess, and buy everything." |
| 6–8 | **The Drop:** Noli splashes into the green pool, the ripple ring clears it, the logo flashes | "Meet Noli." |
| 8–11 | A phone slides up in 3D. Values are typed in: pH 6.8, chlorine 0 | "Enter your water values…" |
| 11–14 | The Analysis results screen. "2 parameters to correct" pops in, then the red alert | "Noli spots what's off…" |
| 14–18 | The PRIORITY #1 card zooms up. The range bar marker slides into the green zone | "…and tells you exactly what to fix, and in what order." |
| 18–21 | Back to the receipt: the unnecessary lines get crossed out, line by line | "No more buying products you don't need." |
| 21–23.5 | The pool is crystal blue. Noli gives a thumbs up | "Don't guess. **Noli knows.**" |
| 23.5–26 | End card | "NoliDrop. Get it on Google Play." |

VO (about 55 words): *A hundred and twenty-four dollars… and your pool is still green? Most pool owners guess, and
buy everything. Meet Noli. Enter your water values… Noli spots what's off, and tells you exactly what to fix, and in
what order. No more buying products you don't need. Don't guess. Noli knows. NoliDrop. Get it on Google Play.*

## Ad 2: "Your Brain on Green Water" (psychology / curiosity hook), about 25 s
**Look:** the opposite of Ad 1. The pool at **night**: deep navy and teal, animated underwater caustic light, neon
cyan accents. Condensed UPPERCASE type with tight tracking that reveals line by line from a mask. Transitions are
ripple distortions and a water-refraction glitch. Moody, then it lifts. Confident, punchy male voice. The music is a
dark pulse that switches to a bright drop at Noli's entrance.

| t | Visual | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | HOOK: an extreme close-up of a glowing brain outline in the water, pulsing red | "Your brain does this every time your pool turns green." |
| 1.5–4 | Five problem tags explode outward: pH, CHLORINE, ALGAE, CLOUDY, STABILIZER, with a shopping cart filling up | "It panics. Five problems. Five products. All at once." |
| 4–7 | The type slams in: **CHOICE OVERLOAD** (a real psychology term, used without numbers) | "Psychologists call it choice overload, and in a pool it gets expensive." |
| 7–9 | **The Drop:** Noli falls through the dark water. The impact flash turns the grade bright cyan and the music switches | "Noli does the opposite." |
| 9–13 | The five chaos tags get sucked into the phone and collapse into ONE card: **PRIORITY #1, pH**. Range bar animation | "One analysis. One priority at a time." |
| 13–16 | A before/after split: left "5 products at once ✗", right "1 step, the right order ✓" | "Fix the first thing first, then the next." |
| 16–20 | Quick 1-second cuts of the Home tiles: "I have a problem" (green water, cloudy water) → Start-up → Winterizing | "Green water. Cloudy water. Start-up. Winterizing. Noli walks you through it." |
| 20–22.5 | Noli winks over calm blue water | "Don't guess. **Noli knows.**" |
| 22.5–25 | End card (night variant) | "NoliDrop. Get it on Google Play." |

VO (about 60 words): *Your brain does this every time your pool turns green. It panics. Five problems. Five
products. All at once. Psychologists call it choice overload, and in a pool it gets expensive. Noli does the
opposite. One analysis. One priority at a time. Fix the first thing first, then the next. Green water. Cloudy
water. Start-up. Winterizing. Noli walks you through it. Don't guess. Noli knows. NoliDrop. Get it on Google Play.*

## Ad 3: UGC "Before you go to the pool store…", about 27 s
**Look:** realistic AI stills of one creator (a woman in her late 20s, sunglasses on her head, by a backyard pool,
golden hour), shot handheld: jump cuts with small punch-ins, handheld shake, and white pop-up word captions with a
cyan highlight word. There are POV shots of a hand holding a phone at the pool edge, with the vector app screens
composited onto the phone. Noli pops out of the phone as a sticker-style overlay. Casual, young, conversational
female voice. The music is a light lo-fi/indie bed, kept low. **No personal results claims:** the creator
demonstrates the app and doesn't testify that it saved them money or fixed their pool.

## Build plan once approved
- Renderer: a new `ads/nolidrop/make_ad.py` that renders PIL/numpy frames through ffmpeg, following the patterns
  in `longform/make_long.py`. It handles vector UI, splash and ripple masks, caustics, parallax, and the
  Whisper-timed captions.
- Spend: 3 TTS takes plus 1–2 spares (Gemini, 10 a day), and about 20–30 Klein images (≈ $0.10–0.15): Noli in
  about 8 poses, the green and blue pools, the receipt and tubs, about 8 creator and POV-hand shots for Ad 3.
- Music and SFX come from the repo's Mixkit library. More Mixkit beds will be added if nothing upbeat fits.
