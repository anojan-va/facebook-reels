The world you see is a reflection of you. There was a house with a thousand mirrors. A happy little dog walked in, wagging its tail. A thousand dogs wagged back. It left thinking, "What a friendly place." Later, an angry dog walked in, growling. A thousand dogs growled back. It ran out thinking, "What a terrible place." Same house. Same mirrors. Different dogs. If everyone around you seems angry, check what you're bringing into the room. The world is a mirror. Smile first.
