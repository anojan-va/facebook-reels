The one who wins is often the quietest one. A farmer lost his precious watch in a huge barn full of hay. He promised a reward to whoever found it. The children searched everywhere, shouting and running. Nothing. One by one, they gave up. Then a little boy asked for one more chance. He went in alone, sat down, and stayed silent. Soon he heard it. Tick... tick... tick. He followed the sound and found the watch. A calm mind finds answers a noisy one can't. Sometimes, all you need is silence.
