You might be sitting on a treasure right now. A beggar sat on an old wooden box by the road for thirty years, asking for coins. One day, a stranger walked by. "I have nothing to give. But what's in that box you're sitting on?" "Nothing. It's just an old box." "Have you ever looked inside?" "No. Why would I?" The stranger insisted. The beggar pried open the lid. It was full of gold. For thirty years, he begged while sitting on treasure. Stop looking outside for what's already inside you.
