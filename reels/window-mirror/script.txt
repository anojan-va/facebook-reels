A rich man visited a wise old man. The old man took him to a window and asked, "What do you see?" "I see people. Families. Life." Then he took him to a mirror. "And now?" "Only myself." The old man said, "Both are made of glass. But add a little silver, and you stop seeing others." Money is not bad. But when it covers your eyes, you only see yourself. Stay a window, not a mirror.
