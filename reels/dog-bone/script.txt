Greed will make you lose what you already have. A dog found a big juicy bone and ran home with it in his mouth. He crossed a little bridge over a river. Looking down, he saw another dog in the water holding an even bigger bone. He wanted that one too. So he barked at it. And when he opened his mouth, his bone fell into the river and sank. There was no other dog. It was only his reflection. He went home with nothing. Be grateful for what you have, or you might lose it chasing what isn't real.
