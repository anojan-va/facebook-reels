The real treasure is never where you think. An old farmer was dying. He told his lazy sons, "There's treasure buried in our field." After he died, the sons dug up the whole field. Every corner. They found nothing. Angry and tired, they planted wheat, since the soil was already turned. That year, their harvest was the biggest in the village. They sold it and became rich. Then they understood. The treasure was the work. Stop looking for shortcuts. Your treasure is in the effort.
