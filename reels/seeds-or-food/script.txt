During a famine, a king gave two farmers one sack of seeds each. The first farmer was hungry, so he cooked the seeds and ate well that night. The second farmer was just as hungry, but he planted them. For weeks he ate less, and his friend laughed at him. Months later, the first farmer's sack was empty. The second farmer's field was full. Same seeds. Same hunger. Different choice. What you sacrifice today decides what you harvest tomorrow.
