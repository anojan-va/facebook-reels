Be careful of people who only tell you what you want to hear. A crow found a piece of cheese and flew up to a branch. A hungry fox saw her. "What a beautiful bird," said the fox. "Your feathers shine. Your eyes sparkle. I bet your voice is the most beautiful of all. Won't you sing for me?" The crow felt so proud. She opened her beak to sing. The cheese fell straight down. The fox caught it and ran away laughing. Flattery is often bait. When someone praises you too sweetly, check what they're trying to take.
