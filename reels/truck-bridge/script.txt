Sometimes the smartest answer comes from the simplest mind. A big truck got stuck under a low bridge. Its roof was jammed tight. Traffic stopped for miles. Engineers came. They argued about cutting the bridge or breaking the truck. Hours passed. Nobody knew what to do. Then a little girl in a car nearby said, "Why don't you just let some air out of the tyres?" Everyone went quiet. They let the air out. The truck dropped a few centimetres and rolled free. When you're stuck, stop overthinking. Look for the simple answer.
