Everything you're searching for is already in you. A young man travelled for years looking for God. He crossed mountains and deserts. He visited every temple. Finally, an old monk asked him, "Why are you searching so hard?" "Because I want to find God." The monk handed him a mirror. The young man looked into it and saw only himself. "I don't understand." The monk smiled. "You walked a thousand miles to find what was with you the whole time." Stop searching everywhere. Look within.
