Stop asking why you were made this way. A baby camel asked his mother, "Why do we have these big humps?" "To store water, so we can survive the desert." "And why these long eyelashes?" "To protect our eyes from the sandstorms." "And why these wide feet?" "So we don't sink in the sand." The baby thought for a moment. "Then why are we stuck in a zoo?" Your strengths are useless in the wrong place. If you feel trapped, maybe you're not weak. Maybe you're in the wrong desert.
