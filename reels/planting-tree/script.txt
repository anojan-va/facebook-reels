The best things you do might be for people you'll never meet. An old man was planting a small mango tree. A young man laughed. "You're ninety! You'll never eat its fruit." The old man kept digging. "When I was a boy, I ate mangoes from trees I didn't plant. Someone planted them for me." He patted the soil gently. "Now I'm planting for someone else." Years later, the young man sat under that tree with his children, eating its fruit. And he finally understood. Plant trees whose shade you'll never sit in.
