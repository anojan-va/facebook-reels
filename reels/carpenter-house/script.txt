An old carpenter told his boss he wanted to retire. His boss said, "Please, build just one last house." His heart wasn't in it. He used cheap wood and rushed every job. Soon, the house was done. The boss gave him the keys and said, "This is my gift to you." He was shocked. If he had known it was his own home, he would have built it differently. We're the same. Every lazy day, every shortcut, we're building our own life. Build it as if you'll live in it. Because you will.
