The smallest one in the room is not always the weakest. A lion was sleeping when a tiny mouse ran over his face. The lion caught him, ready to eat him. "Please let me go! One day, I'll help you." The lion laughed. "How could a tiny mouse ever help me?" But he let him go. Weeks later, hunters caught the lion in a strong rope net. He roared, but couldn't escape. Then the mouse appeared. He chewed through the ropes, one by one, until the lion was free. Never underestimate anyone. Even the smallest friend can save you.
