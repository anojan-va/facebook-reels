Anyone can have an idea. Very few will act on it. The mice were tired of being hunted by the cat. So they held a big meeting. One young mouse stood up. "I have a plan. We tie a bell around the cat's neck. Then we'll always hear it coming." Everyone cheered. What a brilliant idea! Then an old mouse asked one quiet question. "Who will put the bell on the cat?" Silence. Nobody moved. Nobody volunteered. Ideas don't change anything. The person brave enough to act does. Be that person.
