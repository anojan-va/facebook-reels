This is how strong people are defeated. Four oxen lived together in a field. A lion wanted to eat them. But whenever he came close, they stood back to back, horns facing out. He couldn't attack. So the lion became clever. He whispered lies to each ox about the others. Slowly, they began to argue. Then they stopped talking. Finally, each ox went to graze alone in a different corner of the field. One by one, the lion hunted them all. Don't let anyone divide you from your people. United, you are safe.
