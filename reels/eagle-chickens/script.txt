A farmer once put an eagle's egg among chickens. The eagle grew up thinking it was a chicken. It walked like them, ate like them, lived like them. One day, it saw a bird flying high above the mountains. "What is that?" it asked. The chickens said, "That's the eagle, king of the sky. But we're just chickens." So it never tried. It lived and died as a chicken. Who you spend time with decides what you believe you can do. If you want to fly, stop living with those who only know the ground.
