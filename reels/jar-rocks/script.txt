Most people fill their life in the wrong order. A professor put big rocks into a glass jar. "Is it full?" The students said yes. He poured in small pebbles. They rolled between the rocks. "Full now?" "Yes." Then he poured in sand. It filled every gap. "The rocks are your family, your health, your dreams. The pebbles are your job and your things. The sand is everything small. If you fill the jar with sand first, there's no room for the rocks." Put the big rocks in first. Everything else will find its place.
