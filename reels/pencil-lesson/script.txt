Everything you need to know about life is inside a pencil. A grandmother was writing a letter. Her grandson asked, "What are you writing?" "Something more important than the words. I'm writing with a pencil." She smiled. "First, it can do great things, but only if it lets a hand guide it. Second, it hurts to be sharpened, but it writes better after. Third, it can erase its mistakes. And fourth, what matters most is not the wood outside. It's what's inside." Be like a pencil. Leave your mark.
