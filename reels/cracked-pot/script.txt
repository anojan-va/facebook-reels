A man carried water every day in two pots. One pot was perfect. The other had a crack and leaked half its water. For two years, the cracked pot felt ashamed. One day it said, "I'm sorry. I'm useless." The man smiled and said, "Look at the path." Flowers were growing only on its side. "I planted seeds there. Every day, you watered them." What you call a weakness may be exactly what makes you different.
