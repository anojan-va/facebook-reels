This boy taught his rich father what wealth really means. A rich father took his son to the countryside to show him how poor people live. They stayed a few days with a poor family. On the way home, the father asked, "So, what did you learn?" "We have one dog. They have four. We have a pool. They have a whole river. We have lamps. They have the stars. We buy our food. They grow theirs." The father was speechless. The boy added, "Thanks, Dad, for showing me how poor we are." Wealth isn't what you own. It's what you see.
