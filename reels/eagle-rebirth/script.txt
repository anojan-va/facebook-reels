Sometimes you have to break yourself to survive. An old eagle's claws grew weak. Its beak became long and bent. Its feathers grew heavy. It couldn't hunt anymore. It had two choices. Die, or go through a painful change. The eagle flew to a mountaintop. It struck its old beak against a rock until it broke off. It waited for a new one to grow. Then it pulled out its old claws. Then its heavy feathers. Five months later, it flew again, stronger than ever. Growth hurts. But staying the same hurts more.
