She's heard "you're beautiful" a thousand times. Say this instead. Generic compliments feel like copy-paste. Her brain just ignores them. Instead, compliment something she chose, not something she was born with. "You laugh with your whole face. It's kind of contagious." Specific means you actually noticed her. And being noticed is rare. Then don't wait for a reaction. Just keep the conversation going. Not a line. Just honest attention. Follow for more.
