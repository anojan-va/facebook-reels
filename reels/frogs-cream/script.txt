This tiny story could change how you face problems. Two frogs fell into a deep bucket of cream. The walls were too slippery to climb. The first frog said, "It's hopeless," stopped swimming, and sank. The second frog was just as tired. But he refused to give up. He kicked. And kicked. And kicked. Hours passed. Slowly, his kicking churned the cream into butter. It became solid. He climbed on top and jumped out. When you feel like quitting, keep kicking. Your problem might turn into the ground you stand on.
