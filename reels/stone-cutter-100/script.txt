You might be one hit away from breaking through. A stonecutter hit a giant rock with his hammer. Once. Nothing. Ten times. Nothing. Fifty times. Not even a crack. People laughed. "You're wasting your time." He kept going. On the hundred and first blow, the rock split clean in two. The people said, "Wow, one lucky hit." But he knew. It wasn't the last blow that broke it. It was all hundred before it. Don't quit because you can't see the crack yet. Keep hitting.
