Have you ever noticed something strange about crabs? Put one crab in a bucket, and it climbs out easily. Put many crabs together, and none of them escape. Every time one climbs, the others pull it down. People are the same. When you try to grow, some will call you proud. Some will say you've changed. Not because you're wrong, but because your growth reminds them of their own fear. Love them. But don't stay in the bucket.
