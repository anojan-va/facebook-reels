Honesty might be the most valuable thing you own. A poor woodcutter dropped his only axe into a deep river. He sat down and cried. A river spirit rose from the water holding a golden axe. "Is this yours?" "No." The spirit brought a silver axe. "Is this yours?" "No." Then the spirit brought his old iron axe. "Yes! That's mine!" The spirit smiled and gave him all three axes. When you're honest, you may lose a little today. But you'll gain more than gold tomorrow.
