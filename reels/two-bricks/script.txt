You are judging yourself completely wrong. A young monk built his first brick wall. He worked slowly, placing every brick with care. When it was finished, he saw two bricks crooked. He was ashamed. He wanted to tear the whole wall down. One day, a visitor said, "What a beautiful wall." "Can't you see the two bad bricks?" "Yes. But I also see nine hundred and ninety-eight good ones." The monk looked again and cried. You are not your two bad bricks. Look at the whole wall.
