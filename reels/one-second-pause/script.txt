Stop answering questions so fast. It's killing your attraction. Watch what happens when she asks him something. She asks, so what do you do? And he answers instantly, nervous, too fast. Now rewind. Same question. This time he pauses for one second, smiles slightly, then answers slowly. That one second tells her brain: he's not trying to impress me. He's comfortable. Confidence isn't what you say. It's how unhurried you say it. Try it today and watch the difference. Follow for more.
