The difference between heaven and hell might shock you. A man asked to see both. First, he was shown hell. A huge table full of delicious food. But everyone was starving. Their spoons were longer than their arms, so they couldn't reach their own mouths. Then he was shown heaven. The same table. The same food. The same long spoons. But everyone was happy and full. Because in heaven, they used their long spoons to feed each other. Same world. Same problem. The only difference was how they treated each other.
