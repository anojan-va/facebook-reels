A wise man stood before a crowd and told them a joke. Everyone laughed loudly. A minute later, he told the same joke again. Only a few people laughed. Then he told it a third time. Nobody laughed. He said, "You can't laugh at the same joke again and again." "So why do you keep crying over the same problem again and again?" The pain was real, but every time you replay it, you choose to feel it again. Learn the lesson. Then let it go.
