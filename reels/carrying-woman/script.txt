Two monks reached a river where a woman couldn't cross. The old monk carried her across and set her down. They walked on for hours. The young monk was silent but upset. Finally he said, "We're not supposed to touch women. Why did you carry her?" The old monk said, "I put her down at the river." "Why are you still carrying her?" We all do this. We carry moments that ended long ago. Put it down. It's only heavy because you won't let go.
