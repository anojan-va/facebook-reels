Before you repeat what you heard, ask yourself three questions. A man ran to Socrates. "I have news about your friend!" "Wait. First, is it true?" "Well, I just heard it." "Second, is it good?" "No, it's quite bad." "Third, is it useful to me?" "Not really." Socrates smiled. "If it's not true, not good and not useful, why tell me at all?" The man went quiet. Most drama would disappear if we asked these three questions. True. Good. Useful. Or stay silent.
