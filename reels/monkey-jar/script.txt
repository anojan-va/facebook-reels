Your problem might be that you won't let go. Hunters had a clever way to catch monkeys. They put a banana inside a jar with a narrow neck. A monkey reached in and grabbed it. But with its fist closed around the banana, its hand couldn't come out. The hunters came closer. The monkey screamed, but it would not open its hand. It was free the whole time. All it had to do was let go. What are you holding on to that keeps you trapped? Open your hand.
