A king asked his wise men for one thing. "Give me words that will make me happy when I'm sad, and calm when I'm happy." For weeks they had no answer. Then an old servant gave him a ring. On it were four words: "This too shall pass." In his worst days, it gave him hope. In his best days, it kept him humble. Pain doesn't last. Neither does pride. Whatever you're going through right now, this too shall pass.
