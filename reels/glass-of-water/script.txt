A teacher raised a glass of water and asked, "How heavy is this?" Students guessed: 200 grams, 300 grams. He said, "The weight doesn't matter. What matters is how long I hold it." "One minute, no problem. One hour, my arm will hurt. One day, I won't feel my arm." Your worries are the same. Think about them a little, and nothing happens. Hold them all day, and they'll paralyze you. Put the glass down before you sleep.
