Whatever you send out comes back to you. A little boy was walking in the mountains with his father. He tripped and shouted, "Ow!" From the mountains, a voice shouted back, "Ow!" Surprised, he yelled, "Who are you?" "Who are you?" came the answer. Angry, he screamed, "I hate you!" "I hate you!" His father smiled and shouted, "You are amazing!" The voice answered, "You are amazing!" "Son, this is called an echo. But really, it's life." Give love, and love comes back. Give hate, and hate comes back.
