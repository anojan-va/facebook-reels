A farmer's only horse ran away. Neighbors said, "What bad luck." The farmer said, "Maybe." Days later, the horse came back with three wild horses. "What good luck!" they said. He said, "Maybe." His son tried to ride one, fell, and broke his leg. "Bad luck," they said. "Maybe." Then the army came to take every young man to war. They left his son behind. Good luck? Bad luck? Who knows. Don't let one moment decide how you feel about your whole story.
