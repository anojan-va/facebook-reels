Don't look at her face. Look at her feet. Her smile can lie. Her feet almost never do. When someone's interested, their feet point toward you, even when their body is turned away. But if her feet point toward the exit... she's already mentally leaving the conversation. So if you see that, don't push. Wrap it up with a smile and leave on a good note. Weirdly, that's often when she turns her feet back. Follow for more signs nobody tells you about.
