Your pain is not too big. Your heart is too small. An unhappy young man went to an old master. The master told him to put a handful of salt in a glass of water and drink it. "How does it taste?" "Terrible." Then the master took him to a lake and told him to throw the same salt in and drink. "How does it taste now?" "Fresh." "The pain of life is the same salt. What changes is the container." Stop being a glass. Become a lake.
