A man insulted a wise old trader in front of everyone. The trader said nothing. The man shouted louder. Still nothing. Finally, he left, angry and embarrassed. A boy asked, "Why didn't you answer him?" He said, "If someone gives you a gift and you don't accept it, who does it belong to?" "To the one who gave it." "Exactly. His anger is still his." When you don't react, you keep your power.
