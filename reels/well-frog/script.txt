Your whole world might be smaller than you think. A frog lived at the bottom of a deep well. Every day he looked up at the small circle of sky and thought, "This is the whole world." One day, a turtle from the ocean looked down into the well. "How big is your world?" asked the frog. "Bigger than this well?" The turtle laughed. "The ocean is so big, you can swim for years and never see the end." The frog didn't believe him. He had never seen anything else. Don't let your well decide how big your world is. Climb out and look.
