Four blind men touched an elephant for the first time. The first touched the trunk and said, "An elephant is like a snake." The second touched a leg. "No, it's like a tree." The third touched the ear. "You're wrong, it's like a fan." The fourth touched the tail. "It's a rope!" They began to fight. Each one was sure he was right. A wise man said, "You are all right, and you are all wrong. Each of you touched only one part." Most arguments are just people holding different parts of the same truth. Before you judge, ask yourself what part you can't see.
