Working harder is not always the answer. A young woodcutter got a new job. On the first day, he cut eighteen trees. His boss was amazed. The next day, he worked even harder, but cut only fifteen. Then ten. Then just five. He thought he was getting weak. His boss asked, "When did you last sharpen your axe?" "Sharpen? I had no time. I was too busy cutting trees." Rest is not wasting time. It's sharpening your axe. Take care of yourself, or you'll keep working harder for less.
