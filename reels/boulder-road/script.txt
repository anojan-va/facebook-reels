One night, a king placed a huge rock in the middle of a busy road. Then he hid and watched. Rich traders came and walked around it, complaining, "Why doesn't the king clear this road?" Many people passed. Nobody tried. Then a poor farmer came. He put down his load and pushed with all his strength. Finally, the rock moved. Underneath was a bag of gold, with a note from the king: "This belongs to the one who moved the rock." Everyone saw a problem. Only one man saw a chance. The obstacle in your path might be the gift you're looking for.
