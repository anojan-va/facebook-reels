Stop trying to change the whole world. A king walked barefoot across his kingdom and came back with bleeding feet. Angry, he ordered, "Cover every road in my kingdom with leather!" His ministers panicked. That would take every cow in the land. Then a wise servant stepped forward. "My king, why not just cover your own feet?" The king laughed and made the first pair of shoes. You can't change every person, every road, every problem. But you can change yourself. Wear your shoes.
