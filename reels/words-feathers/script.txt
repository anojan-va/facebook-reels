Be careful. Your words can cut deeper than a knife. A little boy shouted at his friend in anger. He said terrible things. Later, he felt sorry and apologised. But his friend stayed quiet. His grandmother took him outside and gave him a pillow full of feathers. "Climb the hill and scatter them in the wind." He did. "Now go and collect every feather." "That's impossible! The wind took them everywhere." "Words are like feathers. Once they're out, you can never take them all back." Think before you speak.
