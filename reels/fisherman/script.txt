A rich businessman saw a fisherman relaxing by the sea. "Why aren't you fishing?" he asked. "I caught enough for today." "Fish more! Buy a bigger boat. Then many boats. Build a company." "In twenty years, you'll be rich." "And then what?" "Then you can retire, live by the sea, fish a little and spend time with your family." The fisherman smiled. "That's what I'm doing right now." The businessman had nothing to say. Some people chase their whole life for what they already have.
