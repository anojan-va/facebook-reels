Your children will treat you the way you treat your parents. An old grandfather's hands were shaking. He spilled food and broke plates. So his son made him eat alone, in the corner, from a wooden bowl. One evening, the son saw his little boy carving a piece of wood. "What are you making?" "A bowl for you and Mom, for when you get old." The son froze. Tears filled his eyes. That night, he led his father back to the table. And never sent him away again. Children don't listen to what you say. They copy what you do.
