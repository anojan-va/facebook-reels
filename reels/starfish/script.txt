After a storm, thousands of starfish washed up on the beach. A little boy was throwing them back, one by one. A man laughed and said, "There are thousands. You can't make a difference." The boy picked up another starfish. He threw it into the sea and said, "I made a difference to that one." The man stood silent. Then he started helping. Soon, the whole village joined. You can't change the whole world. But you can change someone's world.
