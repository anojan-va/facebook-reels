Watch out for this lie we all tell ourselves. A hungry fox saw a bunch of juicy grapes hanging high on a vine. He jumped to reach them. He missed. He jumped again, higher this time. Still nothing. Again and again he tried, until he was exhausted. Finally, he walked away with his nose in the air. "Those grapes are probably sour anyway." When we fail, it's easy to say we never wanted it. But that lie keeps us from trying again. Be honest about what you want. Then keep reaching.
