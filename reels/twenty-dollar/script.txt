No matter what happens to you, remember this. A speaker held up a twenty-dollar bill. "Who wants this?" Every hand went up. He crumpled it. "Who still wants it?" Every hand stayed up. He threw it on the floor and stepped on it. Now it was dirty and wrinkled. "Now?" Still, every hand stayed up. "It didn't lose its value," he said. "And neither do you." Life may crush you, drop you, or step on you. But you are still worth the same. Never forget your value.
