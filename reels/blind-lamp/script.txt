Why would a blind man carry a lamp? A blind man walked through the village every night holding a lit lamp. People laughed. "Why carry a lamp? You can't even see." He smiled. "It's not for me. It's for you. So you don't walk into me in the dark." The laughing stopped. Sometimes the light you carry isn't for your eyes. Your kindness, your patience, your example, they light the way for others. Keep carrying your lamp, even when nobody understands why.
