A monk saw a scorpion drowning in a river. He picked it up to save it. It stung him. Again it fell into the water. He saved it again. Again, it stung him. A man asked, "Why save something that keeps hurting you?" The monk said, "Stinging is its nature. Helping is mine." "Why should I give up my nature because of its nature?" Stay kind. But learn to use a leaf next time.
