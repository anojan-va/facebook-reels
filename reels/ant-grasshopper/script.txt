What you do in summer decides how you survive winter. All summer long, an ant carried food to its home, one grain at a time. A grasshopper laughed at him. "Why work so hard? Come and sing with me. Enjoy the sun." The ant kept working. The grasshopper kept playing. Then winter came. Snow covered everything. The ant was warm, with plenty of food. The grasshopper was cold and hungry, with nothing at all. Enjoy today. But prepare for tomorrow. Because winter always comes.
