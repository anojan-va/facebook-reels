Your scars might be the most beautiful thing about you. A rich man dropped his favourite tea bowl. It shattered into pieces. He was heartbroken. Instead of throwing it away, a craftsman glued the pieces back together with gold. Every crack became a shining golden line. When the man saw it, he gasped. "It's more beautiful than before." The craftsman smiled. "Because now it has a story." In Japan, this is called Kintsugi. You don't have to hide your broken parts. They're where the gold goes.
