One lie can cost you everything. A shepherd boy was bored watching his sheep. So he shouted, "Wolf! Wolf!" The villagers ran up the hill to help. But there was no wolf. The boy laughed. A few days later, he did it again. "Wolf!" Again, the villagers came running. Again, no wolf. Then one evening, a real wolf came. The boy screamed, "Wolf! Please help!" But this time, nobody came. They thought he was lying again. Trust takes years to build and seconds to break. Never lie, because one day you'll need someone to believe you.
