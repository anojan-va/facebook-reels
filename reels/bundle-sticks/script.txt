Alone you can be broken. Together you are strong. An old father had sons who were always fighting. One day, he gave each son a single stick. "Break it." They snapped them easily. Then he tied a bundle of sticks together with a rope. "Now break this." Each son tried as hard as he could. Nobody could break it. "Alone, anyone can break you," the father said. "But together, nobody can." The brothers looked at each other and stopped fighting. Stay close to the people you love. Together you are unbreakable.
