Smart people don't repeat the same trick forever. A merchant's donkey carried heavy salt across a river every day. One day it slipped and fell into the water. When it stood up, the load was lighter. The salt had melted. The next day, it fell on purpose. The merchant noticed. So the next day, he loaded the donkey with sponges. The donkey fell in again. But this time, the sponges soaked up water. The load became twice as heavy. The same shortcut doesn't work forever. Life changes the load.
