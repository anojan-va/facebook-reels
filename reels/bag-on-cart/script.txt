Why are you still carrying yesterday? A traveller was walking with a heavy bag. A farmer offered him a ride on his cart. The traveller climbed on, but kept the heavy bag on his shoulder. The farmer asked, "Why don't you put the bag down?" "Oh, you're already carrying me. I don't want to make it heavier for you." The farmer laughed. "The cart carries you and the bag either way." Life is carrying you. Stop holding your worries on your shoulders. Put the bag down and enjoy the ride.
