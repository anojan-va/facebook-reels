A girl told her father, "Life is too hard. I want to give up." He said nothing. He just put three pots of water on the fire. In the first, he put a carrot. In the second, an egg. In the third, coffee beans. After twenty minutes, he took them out. "The carrot went in strong and hard, but the boiling water made it weak." "The egg was soft inside, but the heat made it hard." "But the coffee beans did something different. They changed the water." "The boiling water is your problem. So which one are you?" Don't let hard times change you. Change the hard times.
