Someone insults you. Here's the smartest way to respond. An angry man walked up to a calm monk and insulted him for an hour. The monk just listened, smiling. Finally, the man shouted, "Why don't you say anything?" The monk asked, "If someone offers you a gift, and you don't accept it, who does it belong to?" "To the one who offered it." "Exactly. I don't accept your anger. So it still belongs to you." The man walked away speechless. Their insult is their gift. You don't have to take it.
