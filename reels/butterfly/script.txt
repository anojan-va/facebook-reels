A man found a cocoon with a butterfly trying to come out. For hours it struggled through a tiny hole. He felt sorry for it, so he cut the cocoon open to help. It came out easily. But something was wrong. Its wings were weak. It never flew. Not once. He didn't know the struggle was what made its wings strong. Your hard times aren't stopping you. They're building your wings. Without the struggle, you'd never learn to fly.
