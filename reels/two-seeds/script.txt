Fear will keep you safe. And it will keep you small. Two seeds lay side by side in the spring soil. The first said, "I want to grow! I want my roots deep and my leaves touching the sun." And it grew. The second seed said, "If I grow roots, they might hit rocks. If I sprout, bugs might eat me. I'll wait until it's safe." So it waited. A hungry hen came scratching through the soil. She found the seed that was waiting. And ate it. Waiting for perfect means waiting forever. Grow anyway.
