Trouble shows you who your real friends are. Two friends were walking through a forest. Suddenly, a huge bear appeared. One friend climbed a tree as fast as he could and left the other alone. The second man had no time to run. He lay on the ground and held his breath, pretending to be dead. The bear sniffed his ear, then walked away. His friend climbed down. "What did the bear whisper to you?" "He said, never trust a friend who leaves you in danger." When life gets hard, watch who stays.
