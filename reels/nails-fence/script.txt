A boy had a bad temper. His father gave him nails and said, "Every time you get angry, hammer one into the fence." On the first day, he hammered 37 nails. Slowly, he learned to control his anger. Then his father said, "Now remove one nail for every calm day." Weeks later, every nail was gone. His father said, "Look at the holes. The fence will never be the same." Words said in anger are the same. You can say sorry, but the mark stays. Think before you speak. Some holes never close.
