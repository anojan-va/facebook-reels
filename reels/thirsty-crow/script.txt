When the answer seems impossible, think differently. On a hot summer day, a thirsty crow searched everywhere for water. At last, it found a pitcher. But the water was too low. Its beak couldn't reach it. It tried to push the pitcher over. Too heavy. Most birds would have flown away. But the crow looked around and saw small pebbles. One by one, it dropped them into the pitcher. Slowly, the water rose. Higher and higher. Until finally, the crow could drink. Every problem has a way through. You just have to find it.
