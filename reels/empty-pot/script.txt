Sometimes an empty pot is worth more than a garden of flowers. An old emperor needed to choose the next ruler. He gave every child in the kingdom one seed. "Whoever grows the most beautiful flower will be the next emperor." A boy named Ping planted his seed and cared for it every day. But nothing grew. When the day came, all the other children carried beautiful flowers. Ping carried an empty pot. He was ashamed. The emperor smiled. "I boiled every seed. None of them could grow. Only you were honest." Ping became the next emperor. Honesty always wins.
