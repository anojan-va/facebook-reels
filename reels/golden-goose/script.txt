Being impatient can destroy your best opportunity. A poor farmer owned a goose. One morning, he found a shining golden egg in her nest. Every day after that, the goose laid one golden egg. The farmer became rich. But he wanted more. And he wanted it faster. "If she makes gold every day, she must be full of gold inside." So he killed the goose and cut her open. There was nothing inside. No gold. And no more eggs. Some things grow slowly for a reason. Don't destroy what feeds you because you can't wait.
