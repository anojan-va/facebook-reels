The smallest thing you do could save someone's whole family. A man hired a painter to paint his boat. While painting, the painter noticed a small hole and quietly fixed it. Weeks later, the man ran to him with a big reward. "You fixed the hole?" "Yes, but you only paid me to paint." "My children took the boat to sea while I was away. Without your fix, they would have drowned." The painter never knew. Do the small good things, even when no one asks. You may never know whose life you saved.
