A man saw a giant elephant held by one thin rope. He asked the trainer, "Why doesn't it just break free?" "When it was a baby, we tied it with this rope. It pulled and pulled, but it was too small." "One day, it stopped trying." "Now it's strong enough to break it, but it believes it can't." How many of us are still tied to a rope from our past? Your old limits are not your real limits.
