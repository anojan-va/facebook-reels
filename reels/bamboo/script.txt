A farmer planted a bamboo seed. He watered it every day for one year. Nothing. Two years. Nothing. Four years. Still nothing. People called him a fool. What they couldn't see was that the bamboo was building roots. Then, in the fifth year, it grew over 20 meters in just weeks. It didn't grow in five weeks. It grew in five years. If nobody sees your progress yet, don't stop. You're building roots.
