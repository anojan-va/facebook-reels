What if your real value is hidden under mud? Long ago, monks owned a giant golden statue. When an army came, they covered it in thick clay so nobody would steal it. The soldiers walked right past. They saw only an ugly clay statue. For hundreds of years, everyone forgot what was underneath. One day, a young monk noticed a small crack. Through it, he saw a glint of gold. He chipped away the clay, and the whole statue shone. Life covers you in clay: fear, pain, other people's opinions. But the gold was never gone. Start chipping.
