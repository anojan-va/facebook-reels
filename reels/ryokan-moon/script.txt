How would you treat someone who came to rob you? An old monk lived alone in a tiny hut on a mountain. He owned nothing. One night, a thief broke in and found nothing to steal. The monk came home and saw him. "You came all this way. You shouldn't leave with nothing." He took off his own robe and gave it to him. The thief ran away, confused. The monk sat outside, looking at the full moon. "Poor man. I wish I could have given him this beautiful moon." Kindness is the one thing nobody can steal from you.
