Every relationship has thorns. Here's how to survive them. On a freezing winter night, a group of hedgehogs huddled together to stay warm. But their sharp spines pricked each other. So they moved apart. Then they began to freeze. So they moved close again. And got hurt again. Finally, they found the right distance. Close enough to stay warm. Far enough not to hurt each other. That's how they survived the winter. No one is perfect. Learn to live with other people's thorns, and take the warmth they give.
