Your anger is not caused by other people. A man was rowing his boat on a quiet lake in the fog. Suddenly, another boat crashed into his. He was furious. "Watch where you're going!" he shouted. "Are you blind?" But when the fog cleared, he saw the other boat was empty. It had just drifted loose. His anger disappeared. There was no one to blame. The crash was the same. Only his story changed. Every time someone hits your boat, remember: maybe they're just an empty boat.
