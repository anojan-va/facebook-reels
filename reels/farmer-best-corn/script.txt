Want to win? Help the people around you win. A farmer won the prize for the best corn every single year. A reporter found out he shared his best seeds with all his neighbours. "Why would you help your competition?" The farmer smiled. "The wind carries pollen from field to field. If my neighbours grow bad corn, the wind brings it to my field, and my corn gets worse." To grow good corn, he had to help his neighbours grow good corn. Your success is tied to the people around you. Lift them, and you rise.
