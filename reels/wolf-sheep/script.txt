Be careful who you let into your life. A wolf wanted to eat the sheep, but the shepherd was always watching. So the wolf found an old sheepskin and wore it. He walked in with the flock. Nobody noticed. The sheep let him sleep right next to them. That night, the shepherd wanted meat for dinner. He went into the dark field and grabbed the biggest sheep he could find. It was the wolf. The wolf was caught by his own disguise. Not everyone who looks like you is one of you. And lies always find their owner.
