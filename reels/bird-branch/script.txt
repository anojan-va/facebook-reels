Stop depending on the branch. Trust your wings. A bird sat on a thin dry branch. The wind grew stronger, and the branch began to shake. Then it cracked. The other animals watched, worried. "The branch is breaking! The bird will fall!" But the bird didn't panic. It didn't even look down. When the branch broke, the bird simply opened its wings and flew. Its trust was never in the branch. It was in its own wings. Jobs break. People leave. Plans fail. Build your wings, and no branch can scare you.
