Force will never win against kindness. The wind and the sun argued about who was stronger. They saw a man walking in a coat. "Whoever makes him take off his coat wins," said the sun. The wind went first. He blew harder and harder. But the colder it got, the tighter the man held his coat. Then the sun came out. He shone softly and warmly. The man smiled, wiped his forehead and took off his coat. The sun won. You can't force people to change. But warmth makes them open up on their own.
