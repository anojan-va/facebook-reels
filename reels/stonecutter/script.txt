A poor stonecutter worked every day breaking stone. He saw a rich merchant and wished, "I want to be him." His wish came true. Then he saw a king, more powerful. "I want to be the king." He became the king. But the sun burned him. "The sun is stronger. I want to be the sun." A cloud blocked the sun. The wind moved the cloud. But nothing could move the mountain. So he became the mountain. Then he felt something breaking him. It was a stonecutter. You spent your life wanting to be others, while they were never as strong as you.
