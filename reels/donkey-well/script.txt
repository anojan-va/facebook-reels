One evening, an old donkey fell into a deep well. The farmer tried for hours but couldn't pull it out. So he decided to bury it. Everyone began throwing dirt into the well. But every time dirt hit its back, the donkey shook it off and stepped on top of it. Shovel after shovel, it climbed higher. Until it walked right out. People will throw dirt at you too: criticism, gossip, doubt. Shake it off. Step on it. Let it lift you higher.
