Hating someone hurts you more than them. A teacher gave each student a bag and a pile of potatoes. "For every person you refuse to forgive, write their name on a potato and put it in the bag." Some bags got very heavy. "Now carry it everywhere for one week." Every day, the bag felt heavier. The potatoes began to rot and smell. "This is what grudges do," the teacher said. "You carry them, and they rot inside you." Forgive. Not for them. For you. Put the bag down.
