Money you never use is worth nothing. A rich miser sold everything he owned and bought a lump of gold. He buried it in his garden and went every single day just to look at it. One night, a thief dug it up and stole it. The next morning, the miser screamed and cried. His neighbour asked, "Did you ever use that gold?" "No. I only looked at it." "Then bury a stone in that hole and look at it instead. It will do you the same good." What you never use, you never really had.
