This is how most people waste their life without noticing. Put a frog in boiling water, and it jumps out right away. But put it in cool water and heat it slowly... it stays. It gets comfortable. The water gets hotter, and the frog keeps adjusting. By the time it's too hot, it has no strength left to jump. It wasn't the heat that killed it. It was waiting too long to leave. A bad job. A bad habit. A bad relationship. Don't wait until you can't jump. Leave while you still can.
