Before you judge someone, read this. A woman waiting for her flight bought a pack of cookies and sat down. A stranger sat beside her. She took a cookie. Then he took one too. She was furious. Every time she took one, so did he. When only one was left, he broke it in half, gave her one half and walked away smiling. She was so angry. On the plane, she opened her bag. And there was her pack of cookies. Unopened. She had been eating his cookies the whole time. Sometimes the person you're angry at is the one being kind.
