Talent without consistency loses to effort every time. A fast hare laughed at a slow tortoise. "You're so slow. I could beat you sleeping." The tortoise said, "Then let's race." The hare shot ahead and soon couldn't even see the tortoise. So confident, he lay down under a tree for a nap. The tortoise kept walking. Slowly. Step by step. Never stopping. When the hare woke up, he ran as fast as he could. But it was too late. The tortoise had already crossed the finish line. Slow progress is still progress. Keep going.
